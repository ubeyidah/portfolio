Learning sticks when it solves a real problem. If you’ve ever opened a tutorial and felt lost by step 5, you’re not alone. The fastest way to grow is to **build something you actually need**, then fill knowledge gaps as they appear.

## Why real projects accelerate learning

- You get immediate feedback from bugs and users.
- You learn the *exact* parts of the stack you need.
- You build confidence by shipping small wins.

> The best curriculum is the one your product forces you to learn.

## A practical loop that works

1. Pick a small problem you feel daily.
2. Build a tiny solution in public.
3. Iterate based on real usage.

If you’re unsure where to start, pick a workflow that annoys you and fix it. That’s how most real engineering careers begin.
