DLog is a minimalist reflection app. The goal was simple: make journaling feel lightweight, fast, and consistent.

## The design principles

- **One action per screen** to reduce friction.
- **Instant feedback** after a log entry.
- **Soft visuals** to keep attention on the text.

## What early users told me

- “It feels calmer than other journaling apps.”
- “The daily streak makes me show up.”
- “I like that it doesn’t ask too much.”

Small UX choices add up. If your product feels heavy, remove steps until the core action is effortless.
